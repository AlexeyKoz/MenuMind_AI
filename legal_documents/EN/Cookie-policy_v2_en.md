# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: October 28, 2025**

This Cookie Policy explains how Bishul.me ("we", "our" or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and application.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

**Cookies** are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to site owners.

**Similar technologies:**
- **Local Storage:** Stores data in your browser
- **Web Beacons (tracking pixels):** Tiny images used to track user activity
- **Device ID:** Unique identifiers assigned to your device

## 2. Types of Cookies We Use

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled in our systems. They are usually set only in response to your actions equivalent to a request for services, such as setting privacy preferences, logging in, or filling out forms.

**What they do:**
- ✅ Login session management
- ✅ Authentication and security
- ✅ CSRF protection (preventing cross-site request forgery attacks)
- ✅ Load balancing (routing to the correct server)
- ✅ Language and region preferences

**Examples:**
| Cookie Name | Purpose | Lifespan |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `sessionid` | User authentication | Session |
| `csrftoken` | Security protection | 1 year |
| `language` | Language preference (EN/HE/RU) | 1 year |
| `region` | Region settings | 1 year |

**Can be disabled?** ❌ No, these cookies are critical for site functionality.

---

### 2.2 Functional Cookies (Optional)

These cookies allow the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

**What they do:**
- ✅ Remember your preferences (theme, units of measurement)
- ✅ Content personalization
- ✅ Collaboration features (shared shopping lists)
- ✅ Display preferences (grid/list view)
- ✅ AI Coach settings

**Examples:**
| Cookie Name | Purpose | Lifespan |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `theme` | Light/dark theme | 1 year |
| `weight_unit` | Weight unit (kg/lbs) | 1 year |
| `volume_unit` | Volume unit (ml/cups) | 1 year |
| `time_format` | 12h/24h | 1 year |
| `recipe_view` | Grid/list view | 1 year |

**Can be disabled?** ✅ Yes, but some features may not work properly.

---

### 2.3 Analytics/Performance Cookies (Require Consent)

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us understand which pages are most and least popular and see how visitors navigate the site.

**What they do:**
- ✅ Track page visits and navigation patterns
- ✅ Understand user behavior
- ✅ Measure feature effectiveness
- ✅ Identify technical issues
- ✅ Optimize user experience

**Provider: Google Analytics**

**Information collected:**
- Pages visited and time spent on each
- Referral source (where you came from)
- Device type and browser
- General demographic information (anonymous)
- Interaction patterns (clicks, scrolling)

**Examples:**
| Cookie Name | Purpose | Lifespan |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `_ga` | Distinguishes users | 2 years |
| `_gid` | Distinguishes users | 24 hours |
| `_gat` | Request rate limiting | 1 minute |

**Can be disabled?** ✅ Yes, through our consent banner or browser settings.

**Opt-out from Google Analytics:**
- Use browser add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- Disable in our settings: Settings > Privacy > Cookies

## 4. How to Manage Cookies

### 4.1 Through Bishul.me Consent Banner

When you first visit Bishul.me, you will see a cookie consent banner with **symmetrical buttons** (same size and style) for:

- **Accept All:** Allow all cookies (essential, functional, analytics)
- **Reject Optional:** Allow only essential cookies
- **Settings:** Choose which categories to allow

Your choice is saved and can be changed at any time through:  
**Settings > Privacy > Manage Cookies**

### 4.2 Through Browser Settings

**All major browsers allow cookie management:**

**Google Chrome:**
1. Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
2. Select "Block third-party cookies" or "Block all cookies"

**Mozilla Firefox:**
1. Settings > Privacy and Security
2. Select tracking protection level

**Safari (macOS/iOS):**
1. Settings > Privacy
2. Enable "Prevent cross-site tracking"

**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Settings > Cookies and site permissions
2. Manage and delete cookies

**Mobile browsers:**
- Settings similar to desktop versions
- Usually found in Settings > Privacy

### 4.3 Through Our Settings

**In Bishul.me dashboard:**

1. Go to: **Settings > Privacy > Cookies**
2. Toggle categories:
   - ✅ Essential (always enabled)
   - ⚙️ Functional (ON/OFF)
   - 📊 Analytics (ON/OFF)
3. Click **Save Preferences**

**Clear cookies:**
- **Clear All Cookies** button immediately removes all non-essential cookies
- Essential cookies will be recreated on next action

## 5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

### 5.1 Disabling Essential Cookies

**Result:** Website will not function properly.

**Affected features:**
- ❌ Login impossible
- ❌ No preference saving
- ❌ Security features compromised
- ❌ No session persistence
- ❌ Forms may not submit

**Recommendation:** Do not disable essential cookies.

### 5.2 Disabling Functional Cookies

**Result:** Loss of personalization.

**Affected features:**
- ⚠️ Theme resets to default
- ⚠️ Unit preferences not saved
- ⚠️ No recipe view memory
- ⚠️ AI Coach settings not saved
- ⚠️ Language preferences require manual setting each time

**Recommendation:** Enable for better experience.

### 5.3 Disabling Analytics Cookies

**Result:** No impact on functionality.

**What this means:**
- ℹ️ Your usage is not tracked
- ℹ️ We cannot improve experience based on your behavior
- ℹ️ No personalized feature recommendations

**Recommendation:** Your choice, no functionality impact.
