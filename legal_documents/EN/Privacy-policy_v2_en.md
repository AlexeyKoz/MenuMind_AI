# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 28, 2025**

Bishul.Me ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

**Legal Compliance:** This Privacy Policy complies with:
- European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
- Israel's Privacy Protection Law, as amended by Amendment No. 13 (effective August 14, 2025)
- Other applicable data protection laws

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

**Account Information:**
- Name and email address
- Username and password (encrypted)
- Profile information (optional: photo, bio)
- Language preferences (English, Hebrew, Russian)
- Date of birth (for age verification - must be 18+)
- Gender (optional, for personalized recommendations)

**Health and Nutrition Data (Optional, with explicit consent):**
- Height and weight
- Activity level
- Dietary restrictions and preferences
- Food allergies (comma-separated list)
- Daily nutrition goals (calories, protein, carbs, fats)
- Meal timing preferences
- Eating statistics (consumed fats, carbs, protein)

**Dietary Information:**
- Unit preferences (weight unit, volume unit, time format)
- Cuisine preferences
- Cooking skill level
- Budget constraints

**User-Generated Content:**
- Recipes you create, upload, or modify
- Comments and reviews
- Recipe collections and meal plans
- Inventory lists (ingredients you have)
- Shopping lists
- Cooking notes and preferences

**AI Coach Settings (with explicit consent):**
- Permission to view your recipes for meal suggestions
- Permission to view your inventory for ingredient-based suggestions
- Permission to view your shopping lists for pattern analysis
- Coaching frequency preferences (daily/weekly/never)
- Coaching style preferences (supportive/strict/balanced)
- Health goals and metrics tracking

**Communication:**
- Support requests and correspondence
- Feedback and survey responses
- Email communications

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Usage Data:**
- Pages visited and features used
- Recipe views, searches, and interactions
- AI request patterns and history
- Time spent on platform
- Feature engagement metrics
- Navigation paths and click patterns
- Button clicks and form interactions
- Detailed user movement and actions on site

**Device and Technical Information:**
- IP address (masked for analytics)
- Browser type and version
- Device type and operating system
- Screen resolution
- Referring URLs
- Access times and dates
- Location data (city/country level only, derived from IP)

**Cookies and Tracking:**
- Session cookies (essential)
- Analytics cookies (with consent)
- Preference cookies (language, theme)
- See Cookie Policy for full details

### 1.3 AI-Related Data

**AI Interaction Data:**
- Recipe generation prompts and requests
- Translation requests
- Search queries submitted to AI
- AI-generated content history
- Usage patterns for service optimization
- Conversation history with AI Coach

**Data Sent to AI Providers:**

We use the following AI providers for our services:
- **Google Gemini** - Recipe generation, AI Coach recommendations
- **Groq** - Fast AI inference, translation processing
- **Google Translate** - Language translation services

**What we send to AI providers:**
- Your prompts and requests (e.g., "generate Italian pasta recipe")
- Context for personalization (if AI Coach enabled):
  - Dietary restrictions and allergies
  - Nutrition goals (calories, macros)
  - Food preferences
  - Cooking skill level
  - Budget constraints
- Language preferences
- Recipe content for translation

**What we DO NOT send to AI providers:**
- Your name or email address
- Payment information
- Account credentials
- Personal identification numbers
- Exact location data

**AI Provider Privacy:**
- Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Groq: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
- Google Translate: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Model Training:**
- We use anonymized interactions to improve our AI models
- Personal identifiers are removed before analysis
- You can opt out of data collection for model training in Settings

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

### 5.1 Access and Control (GDPR, CCPA, Israel Amendment 13)

**You Have the Right To:**

**Access:** Request a copy of your data
- Format: JSON or CSV export
- Timeframe: Provided within 30 days
- Method: Settings → Legal & Privacy → Export Your Data

**Correct:** Update inaccurate information
- Method: Account settings or contact bishulme@gmail.com

**Delete:** Request account and data deletion
- Method: Settings → Legal & Privacy → Delete Account
- Timeframe: Deleted within 30 days, backups purged within 90 days
- Note: Some data may be retained for legal compliance

**Export:** Download your data (data portability)
- Includes: Recipes, preferences, nutrition logs, account info
- Format: Structured, machine-readable format
- Method: Settings → Export Your Data

**Restrict:** Limit processing of your data
- Method: Contact bishulme@gmail.com
- Example: Restrict data use for marketing while maintaining account

**Object:** Oppose certain data uses
- Method: Opt-out links in emails or bishulme@gmail.com
- Example: Object to direct marketing

**Portability:** Transfer data to another service
- Format: RCIP format for recipes, JSON for other data
- Method: Export functionality in Settings

**Withdraw Consent:** Revoke consent for data processing
- Method: Settings → Privacy Controls
- Note: Some services may become unavailable

### 5.2 Israel-Specific Rights (Amendment 13)

**Right to Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate data (response within 30 days)
