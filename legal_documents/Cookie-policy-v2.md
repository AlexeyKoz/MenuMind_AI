**Legal Compliance:** This Cookie Policy complies with the EU ePrivacy Directive, GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Israel's Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

**Types of Storage Technologies:**
- **Cookies:** Small text files stored by your browser
- **Local Storage:** Browser storage for larger data amounts
- **Session Storage:** Temporary storage for current session only
- **IndexedDB:** Structured data storage for web applications

## 2. How We Use Cookies

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

**Purpose:** Required for basic functionality  
**Cannot be disabled without breaking core features**

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------------|---------|----------|
| `session_id` | User authentication and session management | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Security protection against CSRF attacks | Session |
| `language_pref` | Remember your language choice (EN/HE/RU) | 1 year |
| `theme_mode` | Light/dark mode preference | 1 year |
| `cookie_consent` | Remember your cookie consent choices | 1 year |

**Why These Are Essential:**
- `session_id`: Keeps you logged in while using the site
- `csrf_token`: Prevents malicious attacks on your account
- `language_pref`: Ensures site displays in your chosen language
- `theme_mode`: Maintains your visual preference
- `cookie_consent`: Remembers your cookie choices to avoid repeated prompts

### 2.2 Functional Cookies (Optional - You Control)

**Can be disabled in cookie banner or settings**

**Benefits If Enabled:**

## 4. Browser Controls

### 4.1 How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

**Google Chrome:**
1. Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
2. Choose: "Block all cookies" or "Block third-party cookies"

**Mozilla Firefox:**
1. Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
2. Choose: "Delete cookies and site data when Firefox is closed"

**Safari:**
1. Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies
2. Or: "Prevent cross-site tracking"

**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
2. Choose blocking level

### 4.2 Impact of Blocking All Cookies

**Warning:** If you block all cookies in your browser:
- ❌ You cannot log in to MenuMindAI
- ❌ Your preferences won't be saved
- ❌ Some features won't work
- ❌ Your cookie consent choices won't be remembered

**We recommend:** Use our cookie banner to control optional cookies while keeping essential cookies enabled.

## 5. Global Privacy Control (GPC)

### 5.1 What is GPC?

Global Privacy Control is a browser signal that indicates your preference to opt out of data sharing and selling.

**Supported browsers/extensions:**

- Brave Browser
- Privacy Badger extension

### 5.2 How We Honor GPC

- ✅ No analytics cookies are set

- ✅ Your preference is honored without additional action needed

**You can still manually enable cookies if desired through our cookie banner.**

## 7. Cookie Lifespan

### 7.1 How Long Cookies Last

**Session Cookies:**
- Last only while browser is open
- Deleted when you close browser
- Used for: Login sessions, temporary preferences

**Persistent Cookies:**
- Last for set period (1 month to 2 years)
- Remain after browser closes
- Used for: Saved preferences, consent choices

**Our Cookie Retention:**
- Essential: Session to 1 year
- Functional: 30 days to 1 year
- Analytics: 24 hours to 2 years
- Performance: Session to 1 day

### 7.2 Cookie Refresh

Some cookies are refreshed each time you visit:
- Session cookies: Reset on each login
- Preference cookies: Extended if you keep using them
- Analytics cookies: Renewed to maintain continuity
