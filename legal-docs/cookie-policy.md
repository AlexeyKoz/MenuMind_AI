# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated: October 26, 2025**

This Cookie Policy explains how MenuMindAI uses cookies and similar technologies. By using our service, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

**Types of Storage Technologies:**
- **Cookies:** Small text files
- **Local Storage:** Browser storage for larger data
- **Session Storage:** Temporary storage for current session
- **IndexedDB:** Structured data storage

## 2. How We Use Cookies

### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

**Purpose:** Required for basic functionality  
**Cannot be disabled**

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------------|---------|----------|
| `session_id` | User authentication and session management | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Security protection against attacks | Session |
| `language_pref` | Remember your language choice | 1 year |
| `theme_mode` | Light/dark mode preference | 1 year |

### 2.2 Functional Cookies (Optional)

**Purpose:** Enhanced features and personalization  
**Can be disabled**

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------------|---------|----------|
| `user_preferences` | UI preferences (measurement units, etc.) | 1 year |
| `recipe_view_mode` | Grid/list view preference | 6 months |
| `dietary_filters` | Remember your dietary filter settings | 1 year |
| `search_history` | Recent search suggestions | 30 days |

### 2.3 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

**Purpose:** Understand how you use the service  
**Can be disabled**

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|------------|---------|----------|----------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics user tracking | 2 years | Google |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics session tracking | 2 years | Google |
| `analytics_consent` | Track if user consented to analytics | 1 year | MenuMindAI |

**What We Track:**
- Pages visited
- Features used
- Time spent on platform
- User journey patterns
- Error occurrences

**What We DON'T Track:**
- Personal conversations
- Recipe content details
- Sensitive personal information

### 2.4 Performance Cookies (Optional)

**Purpose:** Optimize service performance  
**Can be disabled**

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|------------|---------|----------|
| `cache_version` | Ensure you get latest content | 1 day |
| `cdn_routing` | Optimize content delivery | Session |
| `load_time_metrics` | Track page load performance | Session |

## 4. Your Cookie Choices

### 4.1 Cookie Consent Manager

**When you first visit:**
- You'll see a cookie consent banner
- Choose which cookies to accept
- Essential cookies always active
- Your choice is remembered

**Change Settings Anytime:**
- Click "Cookie Settings" in footer
- Update your preferences
- Changes take effect immediately

### 4.2 Browser Controls

**Most browsers allow you to:**
- Block all cookies
- Block third-party cookies only
- Delete existing cookies
- Receive alerts before cookies are set

**Browser Instructions:**
- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy > Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings > Privacy > Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings > Privacy > Cookies

### 4.3 Impact of Disabling Cookies

**If you disable cookies:**

✅ **Still Work:**
- Browsing public content
- API access (with tokens)

❌ **May Not Work:**
- Login and authentication
- Remember preferences
- Personalized experience
- Some AI features

### 4.4 Do Not Track

We respect Do Not Track (DNT) signals:
- Analytics cookies disabled automatically
- Third-party tracking blocked
- Essential functionality maintained

**Enable DNT:**
- Most browsers: Privacy settings
- Effect: Reduced tracking

## 5. Cookie Lifespan

### 5.1 Session Cookies

### 5.2 Persistent Cookies
- Remain until expiration or manual deletion

### 5.3 Cookie Refresh
- Some cookies refresh on each visit
- Extends expiration date
- Maintains active preferences
