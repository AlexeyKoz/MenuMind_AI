## DMCA Policy

### Digital Millennium Copyright Act Compliance

BishulMe complies with the **DMCA (17 U.S.C. § 512)** and similar international laws.

**If you believe content on BishulMe infringes your copyright, you can file a DMCA takedown request.**

### How to File a DMCA Takedown Request

**Send written notice to our DMCA agent:**

📧 **Email:** bishulme@gmail.com  
📝 **Subject:** "DMCA Takedown Request"

**Your notice MUST include:**

1. **Identification of copyrighted work:**
   - Describe the work you own
   - Provide link to original source if possible

2. **Identification of infringing material:**
   - Specific URL(s) on BishulMe where infringement occurs
   - Recipe ID or content ID if known

3. **Your contact information:**
   - Full legal name
   - Email address
   - Phone number (optional but helpful)
   - Mailing address

4. **Good faith statement:**
   - "I have a good faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."

5. **Accuracy statement:**
   - "The information in this notice is accurate."

6. **Authority statement:**
   - "I am authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner."

7. **Signature:**
   - Physical or electronic signature

**Example DMCA notice:**
```
To: bishulme@gmail.com
Subject: DMCA Takedown Request

1. Copyrighted work:
   "Amazing Chocolate Cake" recipe published in my cookbook
   "Best Desserts Ever" (ISBN: 1234567890)

2. Infringing material:
   https://bishul.me/recipes/chocolate-cake-123
   (This recipe is copied verbatim from my book)

3. Contact information:
   Name: Jane Doe
   Email: jane@example.com
   Address: 123 Main St, Tel Aviv, Israel

4. Good faith statement:
   I have a good faith belief that the use of the material
   is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

5. Accuracy statement:
   The information in this notice is accurate.

6. Authority statement:
   I am authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

7. Signature:
   /s/ Jane Doe
   Date: January 1, 2025
```

### Our Response Process

**Upon receiving valid DMCA notice:**

1. **Within 24 hours:**
   - We review the notice for completeness
   - We may contact you for clarification

2. **Within 72 hours:**
   - We remove or disable access to allegedly infringing content
   - We notify the user who posted the content
   - We provide counter-notice procedure

3. **Within 7 days:**
   - We confirm removal to copyright holder
   - We document the takedown

**Invalid or incomplete notices:**
- We'll request missing information
- No action taken until notice is complete

### Counter-Notice Procedure

**If your content was removed and you believe it's not infringing:**

You can file a **DMCA counter-notice.**

**Send to:** bishulme@gmail.com  
**Subject:** "DMCA Counter-Notice"

**Counter-notice MUST include:**

1. **Identification of removed content:**
   - URL or description of content removed

2. **Good faith statement:**
   - "I have a good faith belief that the content was removed due to mistake or misidentification."

3. **Consent to jurisdiction:**
   - "I consent to the jurisdiction of Israeli courts."

4. **Your contact information:**
   - Name, email, address

5. **Signature:**
   - Physical or electronic signature

**After receiving counter-notice:**
- We forward it to the original complainant
- If they don't file lawsuit within 10-14 business days
- We may restore the content

### Repeat Infringer Policy

**We terminate accounts of repeat infringers:**

- **First violation:** Warning + content removed
- **Second violation:** 7-day suspension

**What counts as "repeat":**

## Fair Use and Transformative Content

### Fair Use Doctrine

**Some uses of copyrighted material may be "fair use":**
- Educational purposes
- Commentary and criticism
- Parody
- News reporting
- Small excerpts for review

**However:**
- Fair use is complex and fact-specific
- We're not lawyers and can't determine fair use
- DMCA requires we remove content upon valid notice
- You can file counter-notice if you believe use is fair

**BishulMe doesn't make fair use determinations.**

### Recipe Copyright Complexity

**Important note about recipes:**

**Generally NOT copyrightable:**
- ❌ Ingredient lists (facts)
- ❌ Basic cooking procedures (methods)
- ❌ Common recipes (public domain)

**Generally copyrightable:**
- ✅ Original creative descriptions
- ✅ Unique instructions and commentary
- ✅ Photos and images
- ✅ Substantial literary expression

**Gray area:**
- Recipes with extensive creative descriptions
- Exact wording of instructions
- Combinations of elements

**When in doubt:**
- Rewrite recipes in your own words
- Don't copy verbatim
- Give attribution to original sources
- Link to original recipes
