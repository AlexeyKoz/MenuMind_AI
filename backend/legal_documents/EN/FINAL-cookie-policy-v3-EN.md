## 3. Essential Cookies

### What They Do

Essential cookies are **required** for BishulMe to work. Without them, you can't use core features like:
- Logging in
- Staying logged in
- Submitting forms securely
- Accessing your account

**Legal basis:** Necessary for contract performance (GDPR Article 6(1)(b))

### Essential Cookies We Use

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|-------------|---------|----------|------|
| `sessionid` | Keeps you logged in | Session | HTTP-only |
| `csrftoken` | Protects against security attacks | Session | HTTP-only |
| `auth_token` | Remembers authentication | 30 days | Secure |
| `load_balancer` | Routes requests to correct server | Session | Technical |

**Characteristics:**
- ✅ HTTP-only (can't be accessed by JavaScript for security)
- ✅ Secure (transmitted only over HTTPS)
- ✅ SameSite=Strict (prevents cross-site attacks)

**You cannot disable essential cookies.** If you block them, BishulMe won't work properly.

## 8. Global Privacy Control (GPC)

### What Is GPC?

**Global Privacy Control** is a browser signal that tells websites you want to opt out of:
- Cookie tracking
- Data selling
- Cross-site tracking

**Supported browsers:** Firefox, Brave, DuckDuckGo, Edge, Chrome (with extension)

### We Honor GPC

✅ **If your browser sends a GPC signal, we automatically:**
- Reject all non-essential cookies
- Disable analytics tracking
- Respect your privacy preference
- Don't show cookie banner (unless you want to change settings)

### How to Enable GPC

**Browser Settings:**
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy → Global Privacy Control → ON
- **Brave:** Settings → Shields → Global Privacy Control → ON
- **Chrome/Edge:** Install GPC extension

**Learn more:** [globalprivacycontrol.org](https://globalprivacycontrol.org)

## 9. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

### Browser Cookie Controls

**You can also control cookies directly in your browser.**

**Most browsers let you:**
- ✅ Block all cookies (may break websites)
- ✅ Block third-party cookies
- ✅ Delete existing cookies
- ✅ Set cookie expiration preferences

### How to Manage Cookies by Browser

**Google Chrome:**
1. Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
2. Choose your preference
3. Manage exceptions for specific sites

**Firefox:**
1. Settings → Privacy & Security
2. Cookies and Site Data section
3. Manage permissions and data

**Safari:**
1. Preferences → Privacy
2. Choose "Block all cookies" or customize
3. Manage website data

**Edge:**
1. Settings → Cookies and site permissions
2. Manage and delete cookies
3. Set preferences

**Mobile Browsers:**
- Similar settings in app preferences
- Look for "Privacy" or "Cookies" section

### Warning: Blocking All Cookies

**If you block all cookies:**
- ❌ You can't log in to BishulMe
- ❌ Security features won't work
- ❌ Most websites will break

**Better option:** Use our cookie banner to control optional cookies while keeping essential ones.
