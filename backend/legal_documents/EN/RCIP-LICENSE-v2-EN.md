## About RCIP

**RCIP (Recipe Interchange Protocol)** is an open, standardized format for representing recipes in a machine-readable way. RCIP enables:

- ✅ Universal recipe exchange between applications
- ✅ Consistent recipe data structure
- ✅ Easy parsing and processing
- ✅ Multilingual support
- ✅ Nutritional data standardization
- ✅ Equipment and technique specifications

**RCIP** was created to solve the fragmentation in recipe data formats and establish a universal standard for the culinary technology industry.
