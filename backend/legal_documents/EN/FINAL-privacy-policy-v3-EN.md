## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

**Access (Article 15):**
- ✅ Request a copy of your personal data
- ✅ Know how we use your data

**Rectification (Article 16):**
- ✅ Correct inaccurate or incomplete data

**Erasure (Article 17 - "Right to be Forgotten"):**
- ✅ Request deletion of your data
- ✅ We'll delete within 30 days unless legally required to retain

**Data Portability (Article 20):**
- ✅ Export your data in machine-readable format (JSON)
- ✅ Transfer data to another service

**Restriction (Article 18):**
- ✅ Limit how we process your data

**Object (Article 21):**
- ✅ Object to processing for certain purposes

**Automated Decision-Making (Article 22):**
- ✅ Not subject to purely automated decisions (our AI includes human oversight)

### 7.2 CCPA/CPRA Rights (California Users)

California residents have the right to:

**Know:**
- ✅ What personal information we collect
- ✅ How we use it
- ✅ Who we share it with

**Delete:**
- ✅ Request deletion of personal information

**Opt-Out:**
- ✅ Opt out of "sale" of personal information (we don't sell data)
- ✅ Opt out of sharing for targeted advertising (we don't do this)

**Non-Discrimination:**
- ✅ We won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights

**Sensitive Personal Information:**
- ✅ Limit use of sensitive data (health, biometric)

### 7.3 Israel Privacy Rights

Under Israeli Privacy Protection Law (Amendment 13):

**Access:**

**Correction:**
- ✅ Update inaccurate information

**Deletion:**

- ✅ Object to certain processing activities

## 9. Children's Privacy

### 9.1 Age Restriction

**BishulMe is for users 18 and older.**

Why?
- Our AI Coach provides dietary/nutritional guidance not suitable for minors
- We want to avoid risks associated with AI and children's health
- Compliance with COPPA (USA) and similar laws is complex

**We do NOT:**
- ❌ Knowingly collect data from children under 18
- ❌ Market to children
- ❌ Allow minors to create accounts

### 9.2 If We Discover a Minor

If we learn a user is under 18:
1. We'll immediately suspend the account
2. We'll notify the email on file
3. We'll permanently delete all data within 30 days
4. No exceptions

### 9.3 Parents and Guardians

If you believe your child has created an account:
- 📧 Email: bishulme@gmail.com
- 📝 Subject: "Minor Account - Immediate Deletion"
- 🆔 Provide: Child's email address, proof of guardianship

We'll delete the account within 24 hours.
